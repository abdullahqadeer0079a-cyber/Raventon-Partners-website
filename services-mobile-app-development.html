Frequently asked questions

Should I build native or cross-platform?

Most products ship faster and cheaper with a single cross-platform codebase. We recommend native only when you need deep hardware features.

Can you take over an existing app?

Yes. We audit the code first, then fix, refactor or extend it based on what we find.

Do you handle store submission?

We handle the full submission process, including screenshots, listings and review feedback.
