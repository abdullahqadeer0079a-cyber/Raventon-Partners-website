Frequently asked questions

Why do I need a separate landing page for ads?

A page built for one offer and one action converts better than a general website page, which gives visitors many other things to do.

How long does a landing page take?

A new landing page typically takes 1 to 2 weeks including copy, design, build and tracking.

Do you test the pages after launch?

Yes. We run structured A/B tests and use behaviour data to keep improving the conversion rate.
