Frequently asked questions

What ad budget do I need?

It depends on your market and goal. We work backwards from your target cost per lead or sale and the volume you want, then recommend a budget and a test plan.

How soon will I see results?

You will see data in the first couple of weeks. Most accounts need a few weeks of learning and a couple of months of optimisation to reach stable results. We never guarantee specific numbers.

How do you report performance?

You get a live dashboard, a weekly summary and a monthly call where we explain what changed, what we learned and what we will do next.
