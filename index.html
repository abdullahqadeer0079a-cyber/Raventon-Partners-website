FAQ

Questions we often hear

What services does Zenvora offer?

We focus on three things: web development, mobile app development and UI/UX design. Each can be bought separately or as one package.

How much does a project cost?

It depends on scope. After a short discovery call we send a fixed, itemised quote so you know the cost before any work starts.

How long will my project take?

Websites usually take 3 to 6 weeks and mobile apps 8 to 14 weeks. We share a timeline with milestones at kickoff.

Do you work with clients outside your country?

Yes. We are remote-first and work with clients in 12 countries, with overlap hours that suit your time zone.

What happens after launch?

We offer maintenance and support plans covering updates, monitoring, backups and new features.

Ready to build something great?

Tell us about your idea and we will get back within one business day with a clear plan and estimate.
