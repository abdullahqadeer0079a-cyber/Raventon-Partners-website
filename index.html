FAQ

Questions we often hear

How much should I spend on ads?

It depends on your market and what a lead or sale is worth. We work backwards from your target cost per lead and the volume you want, then recommend a budget and a clear test plan.

Should I use Google Ads or Meta Ads?

Google Ads reaches people who are already searching for what you sell. Meta Ads reaches people earlier, based on interests and behaviour. Many businesses use both, and we will recommend the right starting point after a short audit.

How soon will I see results?

You will see data within the first couple of weeks. Stable, optimised results usually take a couple of months. We do not promise specific numbers, but we do promise honest reporting.

Who owns the ad accounts?

You do. Your ad accounts, pixels and data stay in your name and we are given access to manage them.

How do you report performance?

You get a live dashboard, a weekly summary and a monthly strategy call covering results, learnings and next steps.
