Frequently asked questions

Should I advertise on Meta or Google first?

Google captures people who are already searching. Meta creates demand and reaches people earlier. Many businesses use both, and we can recommend the order based on your offer and budget.

Do you create the ad creative as well?

Yes. We write the copy and produce static, carousel and short video ads, and we test several versions to find what performs.

Why do my Meta ads stop working after a while?

Audiences get tired of seeing the same ad. We watch frequency and performance and refresh creative on a regular schedule.
